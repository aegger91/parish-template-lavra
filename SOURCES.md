# Sources and credits

## Images

Every image in `public/images/` and the sample audio in `public/media/` are original works drawn for this template by `scripts/generate-art.mts`. No photographs or third-party artwork are included, so there is nothing to credit. Replace them with your parish's own photos or with public-domain images. If you use Wikimedia Commons, list each file here with its author, license and page URL.

Drawing rules followed:

- The Orthodox cross is one shared drawing (`src/components/orthodox/cross-geometry.ts`), used by the site components and by the art script. The slanted footrest rises on the viewer's left.
- Domes and churches follow Serbian Morava school forms.
- Inscriptions read IC XC and NI KA, ΜΡ ΘΥ for the Theotokos. Icons are never mirrored.
